## Permission justifications (Privacy practices tab)
- **activeTab:** Lets the extension read the current page only after you click its button, and only for that tab.
- **scripting:** Runs the extension's own bundled script in the current page when you click its button, to read the page content it shows you.
- **storage:** Saves the extension's own data and settings on this device (chrome.storage). Nothing is sent anywhere.
- **sidePanel:** Shows your saved jobs and their status in Chrome's side panel, opened by clicking the toolbar icon.
- **alarms:** Schedules the follow-up reminders you set for an application, so they fire on time even when the panel is closed.
- **notifications:** Shows those follow-up reminders as Chrome notifications. No other notifications are sent.
